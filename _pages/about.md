Welcome! I am **Yuqi Ping**, a researcher at **Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen)**.

My research focuses on UAV systems, multimodal large language models, and vision-language navigation. I am interested in intelligent autonomous aerial systems, low-altitude airspace security, communication and control co-design, UAV trajectory planning, and localization for UAV swarms.

Research Interests
======

- UAV systems and autonomous aerial networks
- Multimodal large language models for UAV intelligence
- Vision-language navigation and natural language control
- Communication and control co-design
- UAV swarm localization, tracking, and trajectory planning
- Low-altitude airspace security and intent recognition
